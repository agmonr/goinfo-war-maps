* Card text, quotes, figures and article-derived places in `war/data/*.json`: from Wikipedia (he/en/ar), CC BY-SA 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Each events file lists, in `source`, the articles and the pinned revision ids it quotes; the authors of every article are listed in its page history. Derived data is shared under the same licence.
* The IDF history department PDF (Yesodot 5) is a source of FACTS and short attributed lines only; its text is not part of this repository.
* Base map: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, tiles loaded from the OSM servers by the page. `war/land-mask.json`: Natural Earth 1:10m land, public domain.
