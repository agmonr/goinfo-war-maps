# War pages: the rules (one set for every war page)

Source: every instruction the owner gave on the Six-Day page and the other war pages (10-17 .. 10-26), the git history of `war/templates/battle-map.html`, `war/daynight.js`, `tools/war_build.py`,
`~/agent/.coord/knowledge/war-maps-checklist.md` (items 01-17) and the memory `war-maps-rules`. **Six-Day is the reference. A fix the owner asks for on one war page goes into the shared engine
(`war/templates/battle-map.html`, `war/daynight.js`, `tools/war_*.py`), never into one page's data, so it reaches every war.**
Checks: **AUTO** = a script in `tools/` run by `verify.sh` and `premerge_check.sh` (fails the build); **RIG** = a browser rig on a dev machine (Chrome; Firefox where said), run before a war branch is shown, not part of the static verify;
**EYES** = a human/coordinator review step (no script can judge it). The per-war result of the machine-checkable rules is `war/RULES-STATUS.md` (made by `tools/war_rules_matrix.py`).

## E. Measurement and publishing
| # | Rule | Why | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| R49 | **"נבלם" = a battle: the force is hit until it cannot move = DESTROYED. A card with נבלם/נבלמה/נבלמו (also נעצר + fire/enemy word) shows the fight, the stopped units STOP DEAD at the place (no unit of that side moves past the line afterwards), each leaves its burning wreck marker (>= 3, no invented number), the other side fires at the place; an air-stopped force (Gamla: Skyhawks) shows the strike runs, impacts, wrecks.** "בולמת/בלמה/בלמו/עצרה/מנעה התקדמות" = the defender holds the attacker in place: attacker stops dead AND BOTH sides take heavy losses (wrecks both sides, fire both ways). "מאמץ עליון / בכל הכוח / במחיר כבד" = very heavy casualties: loss marker level 2 (double wreck), heavier sound (boom, boom, crash); a count badge only where the source gives a number. One keyword list for all battle laws: `tools/war_keywords.py` (BATTLE, STOPPED, HOLD, SUPREME). "נעצרת בפקק" (a traffic jam) is not a stop. | the owner 10-26 (R48/R49 in his numbering) | AUTO `test_war_stopped.py` (+ `test_war_battles.py`, `test_war_heavy_losses.py` use the same list), RIG `war_stopped_rig.py` |
| R53 | **A battle card with a place is drawn AT that place: its fire arcs, impact bursts and any force drawn in for it lie within 0.15 deg of the card's own place. Cards worded as taking/retaking/falling to an enemy (נופל בידי, להשתלט, לכיבוש) count as battles.** | the owner 10-26 (R53 in his numbering; the Hermon cards) | AUTO `test_war_anchor.py` (before the fix: YK 22/28, Six-Day 10/39, Kadesh 1/10, Independence 2/20 cards fought away from their place) |
